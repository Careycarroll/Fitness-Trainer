# Hollow Body Hold

1. Lie on your back and press your lower back flat into the floor. That contact is the position — everything else is built around keeping it.
2. Lift your shoulder blades off the floor and extend your arms overhead beside your ears.
3. Lift both legs a few inches off the floor with knees straight and toes pointed.
4. Hold, keeping the lower back pressed down throughout.
5. If your back arches off the floor, bend your knees or bring your arms to your sides until you can hold the flat position.
