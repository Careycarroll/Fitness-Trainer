# Band Overhead Triceps Extension

1. Anchor a band low, or stand on it with one foot.
2. Hold the band with both hands and raise your arms overhead, elbows pointing forward and hands behind your head.
3. Keeping your upper arms vertical and still, straighten your elbows to press your hands toward the ceiling.
4. Lower under control until you feel a stretch across the back of the arm.
5. Only the forearms move. If your elbows flare wide, reduce the tension.
