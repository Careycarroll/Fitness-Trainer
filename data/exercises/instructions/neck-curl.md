# Neck Curl

1. Lie face up on a bench with your head and neck extending past the end.
2. Hold a plate against your forehead with a folded towel underneath, or use both hands for resistance.
3. Lower your head back under control until you feel a stretch at the front of the neck.
4. Curl your chin toward your chest, moving only at the neck.
5. Light load, slow tempo, high reps. Never jerk or bounce this.
