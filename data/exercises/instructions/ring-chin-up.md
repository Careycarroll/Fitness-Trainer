# Ring Chin-Up

1. Hang from rings with your palms turned toward you, arms straight.
2. Brace your trunk and pull until your chin clears the rings.
3. Let the rings rotate as you rise. Do not fight them into a fixed position.
4. Lower under control to a full hang.
5. The supinated grip brings the biceps in harder than a pull-up.
