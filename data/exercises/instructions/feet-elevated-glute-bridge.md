# Feet-Elevated Glute Bridge

1. Lie on your back with your heels on a box or bench, knees bent to roughly 90 degrees.
2. Press your arms flat at your sides for stability.
3. Drive through your heels to lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulder to knee.
4. Squeeze the glutes at the top and hold briefly.
5. Lower under control without letting your hips touch down between reps.
