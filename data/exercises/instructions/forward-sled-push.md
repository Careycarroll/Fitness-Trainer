# Forward Sled Push

1. Load the sled and grip the uprights at chest or hip height.
2. Lean into the sled with straight arms and a flat back, weight forward over your toes.
3. Drive with short powerful steps, extending each leg fully behind you.
4. Keep your hips low and your head neutral. Do not let your back round.
5. Push for the prescribed distance or time, then rest fully before the next effort.
