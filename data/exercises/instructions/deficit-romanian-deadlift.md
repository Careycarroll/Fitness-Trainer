# Deficit Romanian Deadlift

1. Stand on a low box or plates so the bar starts below your normal floor position.
2. Hold the bar at hip level with a shoulder-width grip, knees softly bent.
3. Push your hips back and let the bar travel down your thighs, keeping it in contact with your legs.
4. The deficit lets you go deeper than a floor RDL. Stop when your hamstrings will not lengthen further without your back rounding.
5. Drive your hips forward to stand, squeezing the glutes at the top.
