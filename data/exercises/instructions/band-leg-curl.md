# Band Leg Curl

1. Anchor a band at floor level and lie face down with your feet toward the anchor.
2. Loop the band around one or both ankles and shuffle forward until it is taut.
3. Keeping your hips pressed to the floor, curl your heels toward your glutes.
4. Squeeze the hamstrings at the top, then lower slowly against the band's pull.
5. Do not let your hips lift. If they do, move closer to the anchor.
