# Cyclist Squat

1. Set the bar on your back as for a normal back squat, then stand with your heels on a slant board or plates.
2. Take a narrow stance, roughly hip width, toes forward.
3. Descend with an upright torso, letting the knees travel well forward.
4. Go as deep as you can control. The narrow stance and raised heels bias this hard toward the quads.
5. Use less weight than a back squat. The position is less mechanically forgiving.
