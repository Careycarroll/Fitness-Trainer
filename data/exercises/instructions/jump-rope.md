# Jump Rope

1. Set the rope length by standing on the middle — the handles should reach roughly to your armpits.
2. Hold the handles at hip height, elbows close to your sides.
3. Turn the rope with your wrists, not your arms or shoulders.
4. Jump just high enough to clear the rope, landing softly on the balls of your feet with knees slightly bent.
5. Work for the prescribed time or reps, resting whenever you trip rather than rushing to restart.
