# L-Sit

1. Sit on the floor between parallettes, or on the floor with hands planted beside your hips.
2. Press down hard through your hands and lock your elbows, pushing your shoulders down away from your ears.
3. Lift your hips clear of the floor, then extend both legs straight out in front so your body forms an L.
4. Hold with legs straight and toes pointed for the prescribed time.
5. If you cannot clear the floor with straight legs, tuck your knees and hold that instead — it is the same skill at a shorter lever.
