# Feet-Elevated Pike Push-Up

1. Place your feet on a box and walk your hands back until your hips are stacked high above your shoulders.
2. Your body forms an inverted V, weight mostly over the hands.
3. Lower the crown of your head toward the floor between your hands, elbows tracking forward.
4. Press back up to full lockout, keeping the hips high throughout.
5. The higher the box, the more vertical the press and the harder it gets.
