# Ring Pull-Up

1. Hang from rings set above head height with an overhand or neutral grip, arms straight.
2. Brace your trunk so you do not swing.
3. Pull until your chin clears the rings, allowing them to rotate naturally as you rise.
4. Lower under control to a full hang.
5. The rings rotate, which is what makes this easier on the shoulders and harder to stabilise than a fixed bar.
