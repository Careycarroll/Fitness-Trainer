# Standing Single-Leg Curl

1. Stand in the standing leg curl machine with the pad against the back of one ankle.
2. Hold the handles and keep your hips pressed against the support.
3. Curl your heel toward your glute, keeping the thigh still.
4. Squeeze at the top, then lower under control through the full range.
5. Do not let your hips travel back. If they do, reduce the weight.
