# Shadow Boxing

1. Stand in a boxing stance: lead foot forward, weight balanced, hands up at cheek height.
2. Move continuously — step, pivot, and change angles rather than standing square and punching.
3. Throw combinations at the prescribed intensity, rotating through the hips and shoulders on each strike.
4. Return your hands to your guard after every punch.
5. Work for the prescribed rounds or time, keeping the movement continuous rather than punching in bursts and resting between.
