# Zercher Carry

1. Set a barbell in a rack at about waist height, or deadlift it to your thighs and lower into position.
2. Hook both elbows under the bar so it rests in the crook of your arms, then clasp your hands together in front of your chest.
3. Stand tall with the bar held tight to your body and your chest up.
4. Walk for the prescribed distance or time, keeping your torso upright against the bar's forward pull.
5. It will be uncomfortable on the arms. Use a towel or sleeves if needed, but do not let the bar slide down toward your wrists.
