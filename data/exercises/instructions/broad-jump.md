# Broad Jump

1. Stand with feet hip-width apart behind a marked line.
2. Hinge at the hips, swing your arms back, and load into a quarter squat.
3. Jump forward as far as you can, driving your arms forward and extending fully through hips, knees and ankles.
4. Land on both feet with soft knees, absorbing the landing rather than sticking it rigidly.
5. Reset completely between reps. This is a power movement — quality over quantity, and stop the set when distance drops off.
