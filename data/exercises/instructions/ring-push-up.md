# Ring Push-Up

1. Set rings just above floor height and grip one in each hand in a push-up position.
2. Brace hard. The rings will want to travel outward, and holding them still is most of the work.
3. Lower your chest between the rings under control.
4. Press back up and turn your palms slightly outward at the top for a stronger chest contraction.
5. Raise the rings to make it easier, lower them to make it harder.
