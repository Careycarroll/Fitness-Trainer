# Kettlebell Swing

1. Stand with feet slightly wider than shoulder width, kettlebell on the floor an arm's length in front of you.
2. Hinge at the hips, grip the handle with both hands, and hike the bell back between your legs.
3. Snap your hips forward explosively to drive the bell up to chest height. The arms only guide it.
4. Let the bell fall as your hips hinge back again, keeping it above knee height on the backswing.
5. This is a hip hinge, not a squat, and not a front raise. The power comes entirely from the hips.
