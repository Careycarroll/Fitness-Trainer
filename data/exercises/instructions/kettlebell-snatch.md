# Kettlebell Snatch

1. Stand with feet shoulder width, kettlebell on the floor about a foot in front of you.
2. Hinge at the hips with a flat back and grip the handle with one hand, thumb pointing back.
3. Hike the bell back between your legs, letting your forearm contact your inner thigh.
4. Snap your hips forward explosively. The bell travels up close to your body, not out in an arc.
5. As it passes chest height, keep your elbow high and let the bell float rather than muscling it.
6. At the top, PUNCH your hand through the handle so the bell rotates around your wrist and settles. Do not let it flip over and slam the back of your forearm. This is the step that goes wrong.
7. Lock out overhead with a straight arm, biceps near your ear, and pause for a beat.
8. Guide the bell down by unlocking the elbow and letting it fall into the next backswing.
9. Complete all reps on one side before switching, or alternate as prescribed.
