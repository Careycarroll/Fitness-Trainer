# Kettlebell Snatch

1. Stand with feet shoulder width, kettlebell on the floor in front of you.
2. Hike the bell back between your legs with one hand, hinging at the hips.
3. Snap the hips forward and pull the bell up close to your body in one continuous motion.
4. As it passes chest height, punch your hand through so the bell rotates around the wrist rather than banging it.
5. Lock out overhead with a straight arm, then guide it back down into the next backswing.
