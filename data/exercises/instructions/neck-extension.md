# Neck Extension

1. Lie face down on a bench with your head and neck extending past the end, or kneel and brace your hands on your thighs.
2. If loading, hold a light plate against the back of your head with both hands, or wear a neck harness.
3. Lower your chin toward your chest slowly and under full control.
4. Extend your neck back to a neutral position — looking forward, not craning upward.
5. Use light load and slow tempo. This is the one place where chasing load is not worth what it risks.
