# Deficit Push-Up

1. Place a dumbbell or low block under each hand and set up in a push-up position.
2. Grip the handles with your hands under your shoulders, body straight from head to heels.
3. Lower your chest below the level of your hands, which is the range a floor push-up cannot reach.
4. Pause at the bottom stretch, then press back up to full lockout.
5. Keep your elbows at roughly 45 degrees, not flared to the sides.
