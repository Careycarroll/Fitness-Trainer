# Frog Pump

1. Lie on your back with the soles of your feet pressed together and your knees dropped out to the sides.
2. Pull your heels in toward your hips until you feel the position load the glutes rather than the hamstrings.
3. Drive your hips up by squeezing your glutes, keeping the soles together throughout.
4. Pause briefly at the top with a hard squeeze, then lower under control without resting your hips fully on the floor.
5. Reps are high by design — this is a pump movement, not a strength movement.
