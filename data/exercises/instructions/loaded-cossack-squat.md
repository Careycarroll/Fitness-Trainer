# Loaded Cossack Squat

1. Stand with a wide stance, toes pointing slightly out, holding a kettlebell or dumbbell at your chest as a counterweight.
2. Shift your weight to one side and sit down into that hip, letting that knee bend while the opposite leg straightens.
3. Let the toes of the straight leg turn up as you descend. Keep the heel of the working leg planted.
4. Descend as far as your hips allow while keeping your chest up, then push back to the centre through the working heel.
5. Alternate sides, or complete all reps on one side, as prescribed.
