# Sliding Leg Curl

1. Lie on your back with your heels on sliders, furniture pads or a towel on a smooth floor.
2. Bridge your hips up so your body forms a straight line from shoulder to knee.
3. Keeping your hips high, slide your heels away from you until your legs are nearly straight.
4. Pull your heels back toward your glutes using the hamstrings, holding the bridge throughout.
5. The moment your hips drop, the set is over.
