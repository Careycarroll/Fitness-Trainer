# Machine Hip Thrust

1. Sit in the hip thrust machine with the pad across your hips and your back against the support.
2. Set your feet flat, shins vertical at the top of the movement.
3. Drive through your heels to extend your hips until your torso and thighs form a line.
4. Squeeze the glutes hard at the top, then lower under control without resting the weight.
5. Do not hyperextend the lower back. The movement ends when the hips are straight.
