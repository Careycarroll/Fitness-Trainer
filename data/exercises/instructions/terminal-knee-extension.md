# Terminal Knee Extension

1. Anchor a band at knee height and step into it so it loops behind the crease of one knee, pulling forward.
2. Step back until the band is taut, with the working leg slightly bent and the foot flat on the floor.
3. Straighten the knee against the band's pull, squeezing the quad hard at full extension.
4. Hold the locked position for a beat, then let the knee bend back under control.
5. Only the knee moves. If your hip is travelling backward, the band is too heavy.
