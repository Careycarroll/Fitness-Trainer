# Push Press

1. Set the bar in a front rack across your shoulders, hands just outside shoulder width, elbows slightly in front of the bar.
2. Stand tall with feet hip width, weight through the middle of the foot.
3. Brace your trunk hard. The dip is only useful if nothing bends but the knees.
4. DIP: bend the knees a few inches, keeping your torso perfectly vertical. Shallow and controlled. This is not a squat, and leaning forward kills the drive.
5. DRIVE: reverse the dip explosively, extending the legs to launch the bar upward.
6. As the bar leaves your shoulders, press it the rest of the way with your arms.
7. Lock out overhead with the bar over the middle of your feet and your head pushed through, not craned back.
8. Lower to the rack under control, absorbing the bar by bending the knees slightly.
