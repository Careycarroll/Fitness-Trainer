# Push Press

1. Set the bar on your front rack, hands just outside shoulder width, elbows slightly forward.
2. Dip by bending the knees a few inches, keeping your torso vertical.
3. Drive explosively out of the dip and use that momentum to press the bar overhead.
4. Lock the bar out over the middle of your feet with your head pushed through.
5. Lower to the rack and reset. The dip is shallow and fast, not a squat.
