# Band-Resisted Push-Up

1. Loop a band across your upper back and hook an end under each palm.
2. Set up in a push-up position with hands under your shoulders and the band taut across your back.
3. Lower your chest to the floor under control, keeping a straight line from head to heels.
4. Press back up. The band resists hardest at lockout, which is where a push-up is normally easiest.
5. Use a heavier band or double it over to add load.
