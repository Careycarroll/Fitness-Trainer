# Band Pallof Press

1. Anchor a band at chest height and stand side-on to it, a step or two away.
2. Hold the band with both hands at your sternum. It will try to rotate you toward the anchor.
3. Brace your trunk and press your hands straight out in front of you.
4. Hold the extended position while resisting the pull, then draw your hands back to your chest.
5. Nothing rotates. The whole point is that you do not move.
