# Seated Band Hip Abduction

1. Sit on a bench with a band looped around both legs just above the knees.
2. Plant your feet and sit tall with a slight forward lean.
3. Press your knees apart against the band as far as your hips allow.
4. Hold the open position briefly, then close under control without letting the band snap.
5. High reps. This is glute medius work.
