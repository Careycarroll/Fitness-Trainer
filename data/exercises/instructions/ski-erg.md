# Ski Erg

1. Stand facing the machine and grip both handles overhead with arms extended.
2. Rise onto the balls of your feet and load your hips back slightly.
3. Pull down explosively by hinging at the hips and driving both arms down past your thighs — the power comes from the hinge, not the arms.
4. Finish with hips extended and hands past your hips, then rise and return the handles overhead under control.
5. Hold the prescribed stroke rate or effort for the prescribed time or distance.
