# Overhead Carry

1. Press a kettlebell, dumbbell or barbell to full lockout overhead.
2. Lock the elbow completely and push your shoulder up into the weight, biceps beside your ear.
3. Brace your trunk and keep your ribs down — do not let your lower back arch to get the weight overhead.
4. Walk with deliberate steps for the prescribed distance or time, eyes forward.
5. Lower under control at the end. If the elbow bends mid-carry, the set is done.
