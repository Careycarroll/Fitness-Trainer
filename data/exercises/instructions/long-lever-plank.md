# Long-Lever Plank

1. Set up in a forearm plank, then walk your elbows forward until they are ahead of your shoulders rather than under them.
2. The further forward your elbows, the harder the position. Start with a few inches.
3. Squeeze your glutes and tuck your pelvis so your lower back flattens rather than sags.
4. Hold for the prescribed time, breathing shallowly but continuously.
5. End the set when your hips start to drop. A sagging long-lever plank loads the lower back instead of the abs.
