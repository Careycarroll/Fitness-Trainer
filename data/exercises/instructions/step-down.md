# Step-Down

1. Stand on a box or step with one foot, the other leg hanging free off the side.
2. Keeping your weight on the standing leg, lower yourself slowly by bending that knee until the heel of the free foot lightly touches the floor.
3. Take three seconds on the way down. The eccentric is the whole point; dropping quickly wastes the set.
4. Tap the floor without transferring weight onto it, then drive back up through the standing leg.
5. Keep your knee tracking over your second toe and your pelvis level — if the free hip drops, lower the box.
