# Standing Band Hip Adduction

1. Anchor a band at ankle height and loop it around the ankle nearest the anchor.
2. Stand side-on and step away until the band is taut, holding something for balance.
3. Pull the working leg across your body against the band's resistance.
4. Squeeze the inner thigh at the end range, then return under control.
5. Keep your torso upright. Only the leg moves.
