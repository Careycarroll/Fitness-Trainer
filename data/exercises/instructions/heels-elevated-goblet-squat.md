# Heels-Elevated Goblet Squat

1. Stand with your heels on a slant board or small plates, toes lower than heels.
2. Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell vertically against your chest, elbows tucked in.
3. Squat straight down, letting your knees travel forward over your toes. That forward travel is the point of the heel lift.
4. Descend as deep as you can with your torso upright and your chest against the weight.
5. Drive up through mid-foot. If your hips shoot back, the heel elevation is not doing its job.
