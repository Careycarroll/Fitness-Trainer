# Band Lat Pulldown

1. Anchor a band overhead and kneel or sit facing the anchor.
2. Grip the band wider than shoulder width with straight arms overhead.
3. Pull your elbows down and back toward your ribs, leading with the elbows rather than the hands.
4. Squeeze your lats at the bottom with your chest up, then return under control.
5. Keep your torso still. Leaning back turns this into a row.
