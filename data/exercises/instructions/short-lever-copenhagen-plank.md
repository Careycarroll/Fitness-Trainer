# Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank

1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor directly under your shoulder.
2. Place the KNEE of your top leg on a bench, rather than the foot. The shorter lever is what makes this the regression.
3. Let the bottom leg hang below the bench, relaxed.
4. Press down through the top knee and lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulder to hip.
5. Hold for the prescribed time. The work is in the adductor of the top leg — if you feel it only in the shoulder, your hips have dropped.
