# B-Stance Romanian Deadlift

1. Stand with a dumbbell in each hand and stagger your feet, one foot back with only the toes touching.
2. Roughly 80 percent of your weight is on the front leg. The rear leg is a kickstand, not a support.
3. Hinge at the hips, pushing them back and letting the dumbbells travel down the front leg.
4. Lower until you feel a strong stretch in the front hamstring, keeping your back flat.
5. Drive the front hip forward to stand. Complete all reps before switching.
