# Band-Assisted Nordic Curl

1. Anchor a band overhead and kneel on a pad with your ankles secured under a bench or held by a partner.
2. Loop the band across your chest or under your arms. It supports you through the hardest part of the descent.
3. Keeping your hips extended and body straight from knee to shoulder, lower yourself forward as slowly as you can.
4. Go as far as you can control, then pull yourself back using your hamstrings, letting the band help.
5. Use a thicker band to make it easier. As you get stronger, drop to a thinner one.
