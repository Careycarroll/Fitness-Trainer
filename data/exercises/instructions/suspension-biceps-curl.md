# Suspension Biceps Curl

1. Hold a handle in each hand, palms up, and lean back with arms extended.
2. Walk your feet forward until you feel enough resistance.
3. Keeping your upper arms fixed and your body straight, curl your hands toward your forehead.
4. Pause, then lower under control until your arms are straight again.
5. Your elbows stay high throughout. If your hips sag, brace harder or step back.
