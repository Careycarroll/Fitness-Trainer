# Med Ball Slam

1. Stand with feet shoulder width, holding a slam ball at chest height.
2. Raise the ball overhead, extending fully through the hips and up onto your toes.
3. Slam the ball down into the floor in front of you as hard as you can, hinging at the hips and following through.
4. Let your arms finish between your legs. Catch or pick up the ball and reset.
5. Use a non-bouncing slam ball. A medicine ball will rebound into your face.
