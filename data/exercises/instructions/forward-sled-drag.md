# Forward Sled Drag

1. Attach a harness or hold straps and face away from the sled.
2. Walk forward against the resistance, leaning slightly into the pull.
3. Take deliberate steps and extend the hip fully on each stride.
4. Keep your torso upright and your core braced.
5. Drag for the prescribed distance. Lighter loads and longer distances train conditioning; heavier and shorter trains the legs.
