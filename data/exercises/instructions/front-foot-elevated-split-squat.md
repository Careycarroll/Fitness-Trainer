# Front-Foot-Elevated Split Squat

1. Place your front foot on a low box or plate, rear foot on the floor behind you.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
3. Lower straight down, letting the rear knee drop below the level of the front foot.
4. The elevation adds range at the bottom, which is why this hits the quad harder than a flat split squat.
5. Drive up through the front heel. Keep the torso upright.
