# Wall Sit

1. Stand with your back flat against a wall and walk your feet forward about two feet.
2. Slide down the wall until your thighs are parallel to the floor and your knees are stacked over your ankles at roughly 90 degrees.
3. Keep your whole back in contact with the wall, arms relaxed at your sides or folded across your chest.
4. Hold for the prescribed time, breathing normally rather than bracing against a held breath.
5. Stand by pressing through your heels. Do not slide further down as you fatigue — shortening the range is ending the set early.
