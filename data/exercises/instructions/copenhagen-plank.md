# Copenhagen Plank

1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor directly beneath your shoulder.
2. Place the foot of your top leg on a bench, the inside of the ankle resting on the surface.
3. Let the bottom leg hang below the bench.
4. Press down through the top foot and lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from shoulder to ankle.
5. Hold for the prescribed time. Move the foot closer to the knee to make it easier.
