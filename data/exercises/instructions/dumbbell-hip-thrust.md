# Dumbbell Hip Thrust

1. Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench, knees bent, feet flat.
2. Rest a dumbbell across your hips, holding it with both hands. Use a pad if it digs in.
3. Drive through your heels to lift your hips until your torso and thighs form a straight line.
4. Squeeze the glutes at the top and tuck your chin so your ribs stay down.
5. Lower under control until your hips are just off the floor, then repeat.
