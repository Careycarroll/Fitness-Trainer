# Side-Lying Hip Raise

1. Lie on your side with your legs stacked and straight.
2. Prop yourself on your forearm with the elbow under your shoulder.
3. Keeping both legs straight, lift the top leg as high as your hip allows.
4. Hold briefly at the top, then lower under control without letting the leg rest.
5. Do not roll backward. The hip stays stacked over the bottom hip.
