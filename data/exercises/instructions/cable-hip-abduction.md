# Cable Hip Abduction

1. Set a cable at ankle height and attach a cuff to the ankle furthest from the machine.
2. Stand side-on, holding the frame for balance, with the working leg crossed slightly in front.
3. Lift the working leg out and away from your body against the cable.
4. Squeeze the outside of the hip at the end range, then return under control.
5. Keep your torso upright. Leaning away turns this into a lateral lean.
