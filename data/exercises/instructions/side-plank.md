# Side Plank

1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor, elbow directly beneath your shoulder.
2. Stack your feet and lift your hips so your body forms a straight line from head to ankles.
3. Keep the top hip stacked over the bottom. Do not roll forward or back.
4. Hold for the prescribed time, breathing normally.
5. Drop to the bottom knee to make it easier, lift the top leg to make it harder.
