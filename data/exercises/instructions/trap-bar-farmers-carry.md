# Trap Bar Farmer's Carry

1. Load a trap bar and stand inside it with feet hip-width apart.
2. Hinge, grip the handles, and stand up by driving through your legs with a flat back.
3. Stand tall with shoulders pulled back and down, arms hanging straight.
4. Walk with short, controlled steps for the prescribed distance or time, keeping your ribs down and your torso from swaying.
5. Set the bar down by hinging, not by rounding your back. The end of a heavy carry is where form goes.
