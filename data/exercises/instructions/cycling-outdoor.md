# Cycling (Outdoor)

1. Set your saddle height so your knee is almost straight at the bottom of the pedal stroke, with a slight bend remaining.
2. Warm up for five minutes at an easy spin before any prescribed intensity.
3. Hold a cadence around 80-100 rpm, changing gear to keep the cadence steady rather than grinding.
4. Ride at the prescribed effort or heart rate for the prescribed time or distance.
5. Spin easy for a few minutes to finish.
