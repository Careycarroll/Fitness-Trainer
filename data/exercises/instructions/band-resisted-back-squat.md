# Band-Resisted Back Squat

1. Loop bands over the bar ends and anchor them to the base of the rack or heavy dumbbells.
2. Unrack the bar as for a normal back squat and set your stance.
3. Descend under control. The bands go slack at the bottom, so the load is lightest there.
4. Drive up hard. Band tension increases as you rise, so you must keep accelerating through the top.
5. Use less bar weight than a straight-weight squat. The bands add substantial load at lockout.
