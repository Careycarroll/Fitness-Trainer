# Weighted Push-Up

1. Set up in a push-up position and have a partner place a plate across your upper back, or use a weight vest.
2. Brace hard so the load does not fold you at the hips.
3. Lower your chest to the floor under control, keeping a straight line from head to heels.
4. Press back up to full lockout.
5. The plate must sit between the shoulder blades. Lower down your back and it will slide.
