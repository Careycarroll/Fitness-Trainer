# Stair Climber

1. Step onto the machine and set the speed to the prescribed effort.
2. Stand tall and let your hands rest lightly on the rails for balance only.
3. Do not lean your weight onto the handrails or lock your arms against them — doing so removes most of the work.
4. Take full steps, driving through the whole foot rather than bouncing on the toes.
5. Hold the prescribed effort for the prescribed time.
