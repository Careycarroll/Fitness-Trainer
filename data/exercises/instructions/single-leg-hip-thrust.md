# Single-Leg Hip Thrust

1. Sit with your upper back on a bench and both feet flat on the floor.
2. Lift one foot and hold that knee toward your chest.
3. Drive through the heel of the working leg to raise your hips until your torso and thigh form a line.
4. Keep your pelvis level. If the free hip drops, lower the range rather than forcing it.
5. Lower under control and complete all reps before switching sides.
