# Suitcase Carry

1. Set a single heavy dumbbell or kettlebell on the floor at one side.
2. Hinge and pick it up with one hand, standing tall with the weight hanging at arm's length like a suitcase.
3. Keep both shoulders level and square. The entire point is resisting the sideways pull, so do not lean away from the load.
4. Walk for the prescribed distance or time with the free arm relaxed at your side.
5. Set it down, switch hands, and repeat for the same distance.
