# Cossack Squat

1. Stand with a wide stance, toes turned slightly out.
2. Shift your weight to one side and sit into that hip, letting the knee bend while the other leg straightens.
3. Let the toes of the straight leg turn up. Keep the working heel planted.
4. Descend as far as your hips allow with your chest up, then push back to centre through the working heel.
5. Hold your arms forward as a counterweight if it helps you stay upright.
