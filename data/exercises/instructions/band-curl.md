# Band Curl

1. Stand on the middle of a resistance band with feet hip width apart, holding a handle or the band itself in each hand, palms forward.
2. Keep your elbows pinned to your sides and curl both hands toward your shoulders.
3. Squeeze hard at the top. Band tension is highest here, which is the opposite of a dumbbell curl and the reason to use one.
4. Lower under control against the band rather than letting it snap back.
5. Step wider on the band to increase tension, narrower to reduce it.
