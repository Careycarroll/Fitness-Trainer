# Pec Deck

1. Set the seat so the handles sit at roughly chest height when you are seated with your back flat against the pad.
2. Take a handle in each hand with your elbows slightly bent and held at that angle throughout.
3. Bring the handles together in front of your chest by squeezing your chest, not by pulling with your arms.
4. Pause with the handles together and hold the contraction for a beat.
5. Return under control until you feel a stretch across the chest, stopping before your shoulders roll forward.
