# Tibialis Raise

1. Stand with your back against a wall and walk your feet out about a foot.
2. Keep your heels planted and your legs straight.
3. Pull your toes up toward your shins as far as you can.
4. Hold the top briefly, then lower under control.
5. This trains the front of the shin, the antagonist to the calf. Walk your feet further out to make it harder.
