# Band Seated Row

1. Sit on the floor with legs extended and loop a band around your feet.
2. Hold one end in each hand, arms straight, torso upright.
3. Pull your hands to your waist, elbows tracking close to your sides.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the finish.
5. Return under control, letting your arms straighten fully without slumping.
