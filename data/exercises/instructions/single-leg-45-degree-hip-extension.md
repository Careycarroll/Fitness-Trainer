# Single-Leg 45-Degree Hip Extension

1. Set up in a 45-degree back extension bench with the pad just below your hip crease.
2. Place one foot on the platform and let the other hang free.
3. Hinge forward at the hips with a flat back until you feel the hamstring lengthen.
4. Drive back up by extending the working hip, squeezing the glute at the top.
5. Stop at a straight line. Do not arch backward past it.
