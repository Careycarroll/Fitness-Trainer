# Slant Board Squat

1. Stand on a slant board with your heels elevated and toes pointing slightly out. Feet about hip to shoulder width.
2. Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell at your chest if loading the movement.
3. Descend slowly and let your knees travel forward over your toes. This is deliberate: the elevated heel is what allows the knee-dominant range this movement exists for.
4. Go as deep as you can while keeping your torso upright and your heels in contact with the board.
5. Drive back up without letting your hips shoot back — if they do, the movement has become a squat and lost its quad emphasis.
