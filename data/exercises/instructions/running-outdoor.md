# Running (Outdoor)

1. Start with five minutes of easy jogging before any prescribed pace work.
2. Run tall with a slight forward lean from the ankles, not the waist.
3. Aim for a cadence around 170-180 steps per minute — shorter, quicker steps rather than reaching out in front of you.
4. Hold the prescribed pace or effort for the prescribed time or distance.
5. Finish with easy jogging or walking rather than stopping abruptly.
