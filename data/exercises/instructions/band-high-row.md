# Band High Row

1. Anchor a band at upper-chest height and stand facing it, arms extended forward.
2. Pull your hands toward your face with elbows high and wide, flaring out to the sides.
3. Squeeze between your shoulder blades and hold briefly.
4. Return under control without letting your shoulders round forward.
5. The high elbow path is what makes this hit rear delts and upper back rather than lats.
