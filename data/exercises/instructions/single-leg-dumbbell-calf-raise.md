# Single-Leg Dumbbell Calf Raise

1. Stand with the ball of one foot on a step or plate, heel hanging off the edge.
2. Hold a dumbbell in the hand on the same side and steady yourself with the other.
3. Let the heel drop below the step until you feel a full stretch.
4. Press up onto the ball of the foot as high as you can and hold the top for a beat.
5. Lower slowly. The stretch at the bottom matters as much as the squeeze at the top.
