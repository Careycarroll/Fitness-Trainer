# Petersen Step-Up

1. Stand facing a low box or step, a few inches high.
2. Place the ball of one foot on the box, heel raised, and keep the other foot flat on the floor behind.
3. Push through the ball of the front foot to rise, letting the rear heel lift as you extend.
4. Lower under control back to the start. The range is short and the calf and vastus medialis do the work.
5. Bodyweight, high reps, slow tempo.
