# Suspension Push-Up

1. Set the straps low and grip a handle in each hand in a push-up position.
2. Brace your trunk and squeeze your glutes so your body forms one line.
3. Lower your chest between the handles under control, resisting the urge to let them drift apart.
4. Press back up and hold the top position steady.
5. The instability is the training effect. Slow tempo beats high reps here.
