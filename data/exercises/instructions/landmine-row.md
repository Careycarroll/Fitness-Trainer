# Landmine Row

1. Load one end of a barbell into a landmine sleeve or wedge it into a corner.
2. Straddle the bar and hinge forward, gripping the sleeve end or a handle attachment.
3. Row the bar to your ribcage, driving your elbow back and squeezing the shoulder blade.
4. Lower under control until your arm is straight, keeping your back flat throughout.
5. The fixed arc means less stabilising than a free barbell row, which is why it suits heavier loading.
