# Bodyweight Calf Raise

1. Stand with the balls of both feet on a step, heels hanging off the edge.
2. Hold a wall or rail lightly for balance.
3. Let your heels drop below the step until you feel a full stretch in the calves.
4. Press up onto your toes as high as you can, pausing at the top.
5. Lower slowly. High reps are appropriate here.
