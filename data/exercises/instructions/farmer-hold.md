# Farmer's Hold

1. Set a heavy dumbbell or kettlebell on the floor at each side.
2. Hinge, grip both, and stand up by driving through your legs with a flat back.
3. Stand tall and completely still with shoulders back and down, arms hanging straight.
4. Hold for the prescribed time. There is no walking — this is a static grip and trunk hold.
5. Set the weights down under control when your grip fails, rather than dropping them from height.
