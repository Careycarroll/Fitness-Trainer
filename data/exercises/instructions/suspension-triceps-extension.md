# Suspension Triceps Extension

1. Hold a handle in each hand and lean forward with arms extended in front of you.
2. Walk your feet back until your body is angled forward and straight.
3. Keeping your upper arms fixed, bend at the elbows to lower your forehead toward your hands.
4. Press back to the start by straightening the elbows, keeping your body rigid.
5. The steeper your lean, the harder it is.
