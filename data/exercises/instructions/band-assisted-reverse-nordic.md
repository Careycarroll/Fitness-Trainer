# Band-Assisted Reverse Nordic

1. Anchor a band in front of you at chest height and kneel on a pad.
2. Hold the band with both hands. It will support you as you lean back.
3. Keeping your hips extended and your glutes squeezed, lean backward from the knees.
4. Go as far as your quads allow while keeping a straight line from knee to shoulder, then pull yourself upright.
5. Do not let your hips fold. The stretch belongs in the front of the thigh, not the lower back.
