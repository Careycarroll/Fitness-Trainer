# Captain's Chair Knee Raise

1. Step into the captain's chair and support yourself on your forearms with your back flat against the pad.
2. Let your legs hang straight down, shoulders pressed down away from your ears.
3. Raise your knees toward your chest by curling your pelvis up, not by simply lifting your thighs.
4. Pause at the top, then lower slowly under control until your legs hang straight again.
5. No swinging. If you need momentum to start the rep, reduce the range rather than kipping into it.
