# Band Shrug

1. Stand on the middle of a band with feet hip width apart, holding an end in each hand at your sides.
2. Keeping your arms straight, shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears.
3. Pause at the top. Do not roll the shoulders.
4. Lower under control until you feel the traps lengthen.
5. Band tension peaks at the top, which is where a shrug is hardest anyway.
