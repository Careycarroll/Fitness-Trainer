# Front Rack Carry

1. Clean two kettlebells or dumbbells to your shoulders, or set a barbell across your front deltoids in a front rack position.
2. Keep your elbows up and forward, wrists stacked, and the weight resting on your shoulders rather than held by your arms.
3. Brace hard. The load sits in front of you, so your trunk is what keeps you upright.
4. Walk with controlled steps for the prescribed distance or time.
5. Breathe shallowly — the rack position restricts the ribcage, and that restriction is part of the training effect.
