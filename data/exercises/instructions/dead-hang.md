# Dead Hang

1. Grip a pull-up bar at roughly shoulder width with an overhand grip.
2. Hang with your arms fully straight and your feet clear of the floor.
3. Let your shoulders relax up toward your ears — this is a passive hang, and the decompression is the point.
4. Hold for the prescribed time, breathing normally.
5. Step down rather than dropping, especially at the end of a long hold when your grip is spent.
