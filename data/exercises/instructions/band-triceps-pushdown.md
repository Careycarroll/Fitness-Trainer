# Band Triceps Pushdown

1. Anchor a band above head height, on a pull-up bar or a door anchor.
2. Face the anchor and grip the band with both hands, elbows tucked at your sides and bent to about 90 degrees.
3. Straighten your elbows to press your hands down toward your thighs, keeping the upper arms still.
4. Squeeze the triceps at full extension, then let the band draw your hands back up under control.
5. Step back from the anchor to add tension.
