# Safety Bar Squat

1. Set the safety squat bar in a rack at upper-chest height. The cambered weight hangs in front, so it will feel heavier than a straight bar at the same load.
2. Step under the bar so the yoke sits across your traps and the pads rest on your shoulders. Grip the handles in front of you, or hold the rack uprights if you want a stricter upright position.
3. Unrack, step back, and set your stance at roughly shoulder width.
4. Brace, then descend under control until your hip crease passes below the top of your knee, or as deep as you can hold position.
5. Drive up through mid-foot. The bar's forward camber pulls you into flexion, so keep your chest actively up throughout — that resistance is the point of the tool.
