# Stationary Bike

1. Set the saddle height so your knee retains a slight bend at the bottom of the stroke.
2. Spin easy for five minutes to warm up.
3. Set the resistance so you can hold 80-100 rpm at the prescribed effort. Resistance and cadence together set the intensity — neither alone does.
4. Hold the prescribed effort for the prescribed time or intervals.
5. Reduce resistance and spin easy to finish.
