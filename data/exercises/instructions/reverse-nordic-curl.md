# Reverse Nordic Curl

1. Kneel on a pad with your feet flat behind you and your torso upright.
2. Squeeze your glutes so your hips stay extended.
3. Lean backward from the knees, keeping a straight line from knee to shoulder.
4. Go as far as your quads allow, then pull yourself upright without folding at the hips.
5. Cross your arms over your chest to make it harder, reach forward to make it easier.
