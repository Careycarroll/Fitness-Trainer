# Heavy Bag Boxing

1. Wrap your hands and put on bag gloves before starting.
2. Stand at arm's length from the bag in a staggered stance, hands up at cheek height.
3. Throw combinations at the prescribed work interval, rotating through the hips rather than reaching with the arms.
4. Keep moving between punches. Do not stand flat-footed.
5. Rest as prescribed between rounds. Technique degrades fast when fatigued.
