# Trap Bar Shrug

1. Stand inside a loaded trap bar and grip the handles at your sides.
2. Stand tall with straight arms and a neutral grip.
3. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears without bending the elbows.
4. Pause at the top, then lower under control until you feel the traps stretch.
5. The neutral grip and centred load let you use more weight than a barbell shrug.
