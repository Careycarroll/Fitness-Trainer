# Battle Ropes

1. Anchor the rope and take one end in each hand, standing far enough back that there is slight slack.
2. Set your feet shoulder-width apart and drop into a quarter squat with your chest up.
3. Drive alternating waves by moving your arms rapidly up and down, keeping your hips loaded and your trunk braced.
4. Keep the waves reaching the anchor. When they die out short of it, your working set has ended.
5. Work for the prescribed time or intervals.
