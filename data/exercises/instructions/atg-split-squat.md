# ATG Split Squat

1. Take a long split stance, front foot flat, rear leg extended behind you.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand or at your chest.
3. Sink straight down and forward, letting the front knee travel well past the toes.
4. Descend until the back of the front thigh meets the calf. Depth is the whole purpose here.
5. Drive back up through the front foot. Start with bodyweight and build range before adding load.
