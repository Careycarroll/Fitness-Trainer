# ATG Split Squat

1. Take a long split stance, considerably longer than a normal lunge, with the front foot flat and the rear leg extended behind resting on the ball of the foot.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, or at your chest for a more upright torso.
3. Sink straight down and slightly forward, allowing the front knee to travel well past the toes.
4. Keep the front heel planted throughout. If it lifts, shorten the stance.
5. Descend until the back of the front thigh rests on the calf. Full depth is the entire purpose; a partial rep trains something else.
6. Drive back up through the front foot, keeping the torso upright.
7. Start with bodyweight and earn the range before adding load. Most people cannot reach depth on day one.
