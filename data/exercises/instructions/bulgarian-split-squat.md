# Bulgarian Split Squat

1. Stand a stride's length in front of a bench, holding a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Place the top of one foot on the bench behind you. The rear leg supports balance only.
3. Lower straight down by bending the front knee until the front thigh is roughly parallel to the floor.
4. Keep your torso upright and your weight through the front heel and mid-foot.
5. Drive up through the front leg. Complete all reps before switching sides.
