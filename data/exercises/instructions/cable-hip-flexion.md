# Cable Hip Flexion

1. Set a cable at floor level and attach a cuff to one ankle.
2. Stand facing away from the machine, holding the frame for balance.
3. Drive the working knee up toward your chest against the cable.
4. Pause at the top, then let the leg return under control without letting the stack touch down.
5. Keep your torso upright. Leaning back does the work for you.
