# Bear Crawl

1. Start on hands and knees, hands under shoulders and knees under hips.
2. Lift your knees an inch off the floor and hold that hover.
3. Crawl forward by moving the opposite hand and foot together, keeping your hips low and level.
4. Keep your back flat. If your hips sway side to side, slow down.
5. Travel for the prescribed distance or time.
